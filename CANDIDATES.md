# Wi-Fi word candidates (draft pass)

One word each, at most 20 characters, describing my Wi-Fi;
judged on funniest and truest.

1. theoretical -- the Wi-Fi exists, as a concept, in the
   plan documents.
2. aspirational -- it has ambitions (gigabit, on the box)
   that the lived experience does not route; the funniest
   because it borrows the language of self-improvement for
   a router.
3. buffering -- the eternal present tense of the
   connection; true, but it describes the symptom.
4. elsewhere -- the signal is strong, in the neighbour's
   flat.
5. weather-dependent -- rain changes the roadmap.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2, "aspirational". "Buffering" names the
symptom and "theoretical" names the ontology, but
"aspirational" names the relationship: the box promised
gigabit, the router believes in itself, and the connection
is forever on a journey of self-improvement that the user
pays for monthly. Funniest because it is the provider's own
marketing word, returned.
